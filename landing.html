Time-aware organizational memory

Atlas remembers what your startup said believed — when, and why it changed.

Slack threads scroll away. Docs go stale. People leave — and the reasoning leaves with them. Atlas is an AI co-founder that keeps every belief your company has held on an append-only record: what changed, what caused it, and what it traded off against. Ask why, and the answer is on the record.

02 · Decision register

Beliefs get revised. The record never does.

Most memory systems overwrite yesterday's answer with today's. Atlas records a new revision instead — struck through, dated, and linked to what replaced it — so the history of your thinking stays queryable, not reconstructed.

Topic: Mobile offline sync Entries: 3 Opened 2026-01-12
REV A 2026-01-12 Superseded → Rev B

Build full offline sync for the mobile app this quarter.

Cause
Early users on flaky connections asked for it repeatedly.
Trigger
Q1 user interviews.
REV B 2026-02-15 Superseded → Rev C

Deprioritize offline sync; ship a read-only cache instead.

Cause
Two enterprise deals blocked on SSO — worth more than the churn risk.
Tension
Traded power-user delight for near-term revenue.
REV C 2026-07-02 Current

Revisit full offline sync in H2, once SSO ships.

Cause
SSO delivered; the two mobile engineers free up in July.
Tension
H2 roadmap already crowded with the billing revamp.
Query → Search index → Fetch revisions → Answer → Record new rev

Ask Atlas "why did we deprioritize offline sync?" and the Strategy Agent walks this exact chain — states, causes, tensions — then records that the question was asked. The inquiry itself becomes part of the history.
